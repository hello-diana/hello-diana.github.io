[MASCOT](/publication/mascot/), our framework for multi-perspective socio-collaborative companions, was accepted to the **EMNLP 2026 Main Conference**. MASCOT uses bi-level optimization to keep agents in character and make multi-agent dialogue more complementary and less sycophantic. Check out the [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14230), [project page](https://hello-diana.github.io/MASCOT/), and [code](https://github.com/hello-diana/MASCOT).
