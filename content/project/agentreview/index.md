Peer review is central to scientific publishing, but its dynamics are hard to study because the data is sensitive and shaped by many latent factors. **AgentReview** is the first LLM-based peer-review simulation framework, disentangling the influence of reviewers, authors, and area chairs while sidestepping privacy concerns. The simulations reveal sizable effects — including a 37.1% variation in paper decisions attributable to reviewer biases — interpreted through sociological theories such as social influence and authority bias.

**My role.** I contributed to this collaboration as a co-author. AgentReview was published at **EMNLP 2024 (Main Track, Oral)**.
