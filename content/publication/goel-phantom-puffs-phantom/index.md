## Abstract

Testing sensors on human subjects is fraught with challenges such as extensive setup times and inconsistent data collection. We explore phantom organs to streamline sensor testing and validation, developing a **phantom lung** capable of emulating human breathing patterns. This provides a consistent, repeatable testing environment for ENDS monitoring sensors, reducing reliance on human subjects while enabling experimentation with novel sensing mechanisms.
