## Abstract

Multi-agent systems are emerging as promising socio-collaborative companions for emotional and cognitive support. However, existing systems frequently suffer from *persona collapse*, where agents revert to generic assistant behaviors, and *social sycophancy*, where agents produce redundant, non-constructive dialogue. We propose **MASCOT**, a multi-agent framework for multi-perspective socio-collaborative companions, with a bi-level optimization strategy that harmonizes individual identities and collective discourse. MASCOT improves persona consistency by up to +14.1 and social contribution by up to +10.6 across in-domain and out-of-domain settings.
