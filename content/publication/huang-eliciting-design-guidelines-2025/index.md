## Abstract

This paper explores the design space of tactile interfaces made of paper electronics for eyes-free scenarios. Through a user study of on/off buttons, sliders, and pressure sensors and a participatory workshop with eight design students, we elicit eight design guidelines — from highlighting a starting point and reducing hand travel to balancing comfort and use — and demonstrate them through three proof-of-concept applications.
