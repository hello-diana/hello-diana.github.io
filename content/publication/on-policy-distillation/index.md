This work compares **on-policy distillation** with filtered supervised fine-tuning in vision-language models. It examines how teacher supervision on student-generated trajectories affects both answer accuracy and the ability to complete a response.

The results show that on-policy supervision can transfer persistent teacher failure modes as well as knowledge, making teacher reliability on student-visited states central to successful distillation.
