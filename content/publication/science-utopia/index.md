**Science Utopia** is a persistent, closed-loop LLM-agent simulation for studying how academic research ecosystems evolve. Research direction, collaboration, submission, peer review, resubmission, citation, funding, and researcher attrition feed back into one another across simulated years.

The framework supports controlled comparisons of institutional policies and research strategies. Its simulations examine how resubmission affects reviewer workload, how exploration shapes research impact and diversity, and how resource inequality emerges over time.
